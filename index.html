We are a small indie game studio from Poland with a passion for making simple video games. We work in our free time, constantly learning new things to improve our projects and share them with you.
We're currently working on our first game, Yavalanche! It's a tower defense game in which you take control of a courageous yeti that lives on top of the mountain to repel waves of the sinister goblins coming to get you. Place your pins, strategize your attacks, upgrade your fortifications, and send those pesky green goblins down! Check it out on Steam!
